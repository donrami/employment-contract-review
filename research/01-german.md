# German Statutory & Case-Law Framework for Employment-Contract Assessment

**As of 2026-08-22.** German term + English gloss. Every claim carries legal basis. All URLs verified 2026-08-22 unless marked `[unverified]`. Currency figures current as of the date shown; annual figures (Pfändungsfreigrenzen, BBG) change 1 Jan/1 Jul.

---

## 1. Norm hierarchy & AGB-Kontrolle

**Hierarchy (highest → lowest):** mandatory statutory law (zwingendes Recht, e.g. MiLoG, ArbZG, most of BUrlG/KSchG) → collective agreement (Tarifvertrag, § 4 TVG) → works agreement (Betriebsvereinbarung, §§ 77, 87 BetrVG) → individual contract. Contract clauses that deviate from mandatory law to the employee's detriment are void (§ 134 BGB) or unenforceable; statutory minimums are floors, not ceilings.

**AGB-Kontrolle (standard-terms review):**
- § 305(1) BGB: AGB = pre-formulated terms for a multitude of contracts (Formulararbeitsvertrag). Employment contracts are subject to full AGB review: § 310(4) S. 2 BGB expressly applies §§ 305–310 BGB to employment contracts, with "im Arbeitsrecht geltende Besonderheiten" (employment-law peculiarities) to be considered in § 307(1)-(2) review. `https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__310.html`
- § 305b BGB: individual agreement (Individualabrede) prevails over AGB — e.g., a hand-written side letter overrides the printed clause.
- § 305c(1) BGB: surprising/overly hidden clauses (überraschende Klauseln) do not become part of the contract (BAG 31.8.2005 – 5 AZR 545/04; BAG 14.8.2007 – 8 AZR 973/06). `https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__305c.html`
- § 305c(2) BGB: ambiguity resolves against the user (Unklarheitenregel).
- § 306 BGB: severability (Teilbarkeit) — if a clause is void, the rest of the contract survives; the void clause is NOT replaced by the statutory default via geltungserhaltende Reduktion (no gap-filling reduction) in AGB cases, except where § 306(2) BGB allows statutory law to step in.
- §§ 307–309 BGB: substantive review (Inhaltskontrolle). § 307(1) BGB: unreasonable disadvantage / § 307(1) S. 2: transparency requirement (Transparenzgebot) — clauses must be clear and understandable (BAG 24.8.2017 – 8 AZR 378/16). § 308 BGB (clauses subject to review), § 309 BGB (clause catalog — per § 310(4) S. 2, § 309 applies to employment contracts except where employment-law peculiarities justify deviation; e.g. penalty clauses: see § 3.2).
- **BAG practice:** clause-by-clause review (Klausel-für-Klausel-Prüfung) — each clause assessed individually for §§ 305c, 307, 308, 309; no "salvatorische Klausel" (severability clause) can cure a void clause.

## 2. Key statutes and contract-relevant provisions

### 2.1 BGB (Bürgerliches Gesetzbuch — Civil Code)
- § 611a BGB: employment contract definition (Dienstvertrag mit Weisungsgebundenheit und persönlicher Abhängigkeit). `https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__611a.html`
- § 612a BGB: prohibition of retaliation/detriment for exercising rights (Maßregelungsverbot).
- § 615 BGB: remuneration in default of acceptance (Annahmeverzug) — employee gets pay if employer fails to accept work.
- § 616 BGB: continued pay for temporary personal obstacles (verhältnismäßig nicht erhebliche Zeit); largely displaced by EFZG/collective agreements.
- § 619 BGB: mandatory nature of §§ 617, 618 (duty of care — cannot be waived in advance).
- §§ 620–623 BGB: contract duration; § 620 — employment ends at term; § 623 — **Schriftform (written form) requirement: any termination (Kündigung) and its revocation is only valid in writing (paper, signed — not e-mail/fax/Textform)**; electronic form excluded. `https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__623.html`
- § 626 BGB: extraordinary termination without notice (fristlose Kündigung) — important cause; § 626(2): 2-week claim period from knowledge. `https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__626.html`
- § 628 BGB: damages for early termination; § 630 BGB: duty to issue a written reference/certificate (Zeugnis).

### 2.2 Nachweisgesetz (NachwG — Evidence Act, implementing EU Directive 2019/1152)
In force 1.8.2022 (BGBl. I 2022, S. 1170). `https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/nachwg/`

**§ 2(1) S. 7 NachwG — mandatory written documentation (Niederschrift) of all of:**
1. Name and address of both parties;
2. Start date of employment;
3. For fixed-term: end date or foreseeable duration;
4. Place of work (or statement that employee works at various places / may choose);
5. Brief characterization/description of the activity;
6. If agreed, probation period duration;
7. **Composition and amount of remuneration, including overtime pay, supplements, allowances, bonuses and special payments — each separately listed — plus due date and method of payment**;
8. Agreed working time, rest breaks and rest periods; for shift work: shift system, rhythm, conditions for changes;
9. For Arbeit auf Abruf (on-call work, § 12 TzBfG): agreement that work follows demand, minimum guaranteed hours, time frame (reference days/hours), and advance-notice period for scheduling;
10. If agreed, possibility of ordering overtime and its conditions;
11. Duration of annual leave;
12. Any entitlement to employer-provided training;
13. For occupational pension via a provider: provider name and address (unless provider itself must inform);
14. **Termination procedure, at minimum the written-form requirement and notice periods for both parties, and the deadline for filing a Kündigungsschutzklage (unfair-dismissal claim); § 7 KSchG applies even if the notice-period info is deficient**;
15. General reference to applicable Tarifverträge, Betriebs-/Dienstvereinbarungen and church-law commission rules.

**Deadlines (§ 2(1) S. 9):** items 1, 7, 8 → on first day of work; items 2–6, 9, 10 → within 7 calendar days; remaining items → within 1 month. Changes must be documented within 1 month of becoming effective. For work abroad >4 consecutive weeks: § 2(2) — additional items (country, duration, currency, benefits, return terms) before departure; posting per Directive 96/71/EC adds § 2(3) items.

**Consequences of non-compliance:**
- § 4 NachwG: employer must provide the Niederschrift on demand; claim is not time-barred before end of the year in which employment ends.
- **§ 4 S. 1 NachwG: Beweislast (burden of proof) — if the employer fails to document agreed terms, the employee's assertion of those terms is presumed correct in litigation** (reversed burden of proof). `https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/nachwg/__4.html`
- Fines: Ordnungswidrigkeit — up to €2,000 per violation (§ 4 S. 1 i.V.m. § 5 NachwG; Bußgeldrahmen); Freshfields: `https://www.freshfields.com/de/unser-denken/briefings/2022/11/bubgelder-nach-dem-nachweisgesetz--bubgeldrisiken-infolge-digitaler-hr-organisation`
- § 2(5) NachwG: a full written employment contract satisfies the documentation requirement insofar as it contains the required items.

### 2.3 TzBfG (Teilzeit- und Befristungsgesetz — Part-Time and Fixed-Term Act)
- § 4 TzBfG: less-favorable treatment of part-time workers prohibited (Diskriminierungsverbot).
- § 8 TzBfG: right to reduce working hours — employer with >15 employees; 3-month lead; can refuse only for operational reasons (§ 8(4)); automatic reduction if not answered within 1 month (Fiktion). `https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/tzbfg/__8.html`
- § 9 TzBfG: right to return to longer hours (Verlängerung); employer must announce vacancies.
- § 9a TzBfG: **Brückenteilzeit (bridge part-time)** — since 1.1.2019 (BGBl. I 2018, S. 2384): employee after >6 months' tenure may demand a *temporary* reduction for a fixed period of 1–5 years; only if employer regularly employs >45 employees; § 9a(2) — refusal only for operational reasons, plus a special quota rule for 46–200 employees (max. one acceptance per 15 employees); no new reduction/extension during the period (§ 9a(4)); re-application earliest 1 year after return (§ 9a(5)). Verified text: `https://dejure.org/gesetze/TzBfG/9a.html`
- § 14(1) TzBfG: fixed term requires a sachlicher Grund (objective reason) — catalog in § 14(1) (temporary need, post-training transition, replacement/Vertretung, nature of work, probation/Erprobung, personal reasons, budget-funded, court settlement). `https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/tzbfg/__14.html`
- § 14(2) TzBfG: **sachgrundlose Befristung (term without objective reason): max 2 years total, max 3 extensions; PROHIBITED if a prior employment relationship (fixed or permanent) with the same employer existed** (Vorbeschäftigungsverbot). BVerfG 6.6.2018 – 1 BvL 7/14, 1 BvR 1375/14 declared the old 3-year "gap" interpretation unconstitutional; BAG 23.1.2019 – 7 AZR 733/16 (and 7 AZR 13/17) held an 8-year-old prior employment blocks sachgrundlose Befristung; BAG 21.8.2019 – 7 AZR 452/17: 22-year-old prior employment does NOT block (unzumutbar exception). A very recent prior employment (e.g. 2022/2023) always blocks. New cases: BAG 12.6.2024 – 7 AZR 203/23 (prior employment of a different kind; Vorbeschäftigung extends the prohibition) `https://www.bundesarbeitsgericht.de/entscheidung/7-azr-203-23/`; BAG 16.8.2023 – 7 AZR 300/22 (oral agreement to start earlier does not waive Schriftform). The spec's "7 AZR 308/22" could not be confirmed — see § 3.5.
- § 14(2a): new companies — 4 years/multiple extensions; § 14(3): employees >52 years after ≥4 months' unemployment — 5 years.
- § 14(4) TzBfG: fixed term requires Schriftform (written form — § 126 BGB, paper signature; e-mail insufficient); otherwise the term is void and an indefinite contract results (§ 16 TzBfG).
- § 15(2) TzBfG: notice of 3 calendar days during fixed term; § 15(3): if term is not stated, 2 years presumed.
- §§ 17–21 TzBfG: Klagefrist — a challenge to the fixed term must be filed within 3 weeks of the agreed end (§ 17 TzBfG; § 21 analogizes § 7 KSchG: late filing = term deemed valid).
- § 12 TzBfG: Arbeit auf Abruf — minimum guaranteed hours (10 h/week floor under § 12(1) S. 4? — actually § 12(1) S. 3: at least 20% of weekly hours must be guaranteed if agreed hours set; default: 20 h/week (§ 12(1) S. 4); 2022 amendment: reference periods, 25% rule for scheduling; employee consent needed for shifts outside agreed windows). `https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/tzbfg/__12.html`

### 2.4 KSchG (Kündigungsschutzgesetz — Unfair-Dismissal Protection Act)
- § 1 KSchG: dismissal socially unjustified unless due to personal, conduct-related, or urgent operational reasons (personen-, verhaltens-, betriebsbedingt).
- **Wartezeit:** § 1(1) KSchG — protection only after >6 months' continuous employment (Wartezeit).
- **Kleinbetriebsklausel:** § 23(1) KSchG — general protection does NOT apply in establishments regularly employing ≤10 employees (≤5 for contracts begun before 1.1.2004); part-time weighting: ≤20 h/week = 0.5, ≤30 h/week = 0.75; trainees excluded. Verified text: `https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/kschg/__23.html`
- §§ 4, 5, 7 KSchG: **Klagefrist — an unfair-dismissal claim must be filed within 3 weeks of receipt of the written termination; § 5: late filing allowed only if the employee was prevented without fault (nachträgliche Zulassung); § 7: after expiry the dismissal is deemed valid** (fiktive Wirksamkeit).
- § 2 KSchG: Änderungskündigung (change/amendment dismissal) — dismissal combined with offer to continue under modified terms; employee must accept under protest (Vorbehalt) within 3-week deadline (§ 2 S. 2) or claim is lost.
- § 23(1) S. 1 i.V.m. § 24: applies to private and public employers.
- Special protection (besonderer Kündigungsschutz): MuSchG §§ 17–18 (pregnant women/mothers — prohibition during pregnancy + 4 months after birth, requires Behördenzustimmung), SGB IX § 168 (severely disabled — requires Integrationsamt consent; § 85 ff. SGB IX), BetrVG § 15 (works council members — extraordinary only), ArbPlSchG § 2 (military reserve duty), PflegeZG § 5 (care leave), BEEG § 18 (parental leave), BAG (Mutterschutz).

### 2.5 BUrlG (Bundesurlaubsgesetz — Federal Leave Act)
- § 1 BUrlG: minimum paid leave each calendar year.
- § 3(1) BUrlG: **minimum 24 Werktage (workdays, Mon–Sat) = 4 weeks for a 6-day week; for a 5-day week: 20 Arbeitstage**.
- § 4 BUrlG: Wartezeit — full leave claim only after 6 months; partial leave (1/12 per month) before that (§ 5).
- § 7(1)-(3) BUrlG: leave must be granted in the calendar year; **Übertragung (carry-over) into the following year only if justified by urgent operational or personal reasons; must be taken by 31.3. of the following year**; § 7(4): monetary compensation only if leave impossible due to termination (Urlaubsabgeltung).
- § 8 BUrlG: illness during leave does not count as leave (only with AU certificate).
- § 11 BUrlG: holiday pay = average remuneration of the preceding 13 weeks (Urlaubsentgelt).
- § 13 BUrlG: **Abweichungsverbot — deviation from §§ 1, 2, 3(1), 6, 7 BUrlG to the employee's detriment is prohibited** (contractual clauses giving less leave are void); only collective agreements may deviate, and only in the employee's favor for § 7(2) Frist.
- 15-month rule: § 7(3) BUrlG read with EU law — BAG 19.2.2019 – 9 AZR 423/16 (BAGE 165, 376): leave expires 15 months after the end of the leave year at the earliest, and only if the employer enabled and prompted the employee (Mitwirkungsobliegenheiten — C-684/16 "Max-Planck" reference); BAG 7.7.2020 – 9 AZR 245/19 (A): employer must specifically urge the employee and warn of expiry; BAG 12.10.2021 – 9 AZR 577/20 (A): no expiry without employer cooperation; for long-term sick: expiry after 15 months even without employer action if the employee was continuously incapacitated (§ 7(3) BUrlG analogously; BAG 10.3.2021 – 9 AZR 199/20). Sources: `https://www.arbeitsrecht-rheinland-pfalz.de/bundesarbeitsgericht/detailansicht/artikel/jahresurlaub-darf-auch-bei-laengerer-krankheit-nicht-einfach-verfallen.html`, `https://www.etl-rechtsanwaelte.de/aktuelles/zur-15-monats-verfallfrist-bei-einem-dauerkranken/`

### 2.6 EFZG (Entgeltfortzahlungsgesetz — Continued-Pay Act)
- § 2 EFZG: pay on public holidays (Feiertage).
- § 3(1) EFZG: **sick pay (Entgeltfortzahlung) for up to 6 weeks per illness**; § 3(1) S. 2–3: renewed claim after 6 months without the same illness or after 12 months.
- § 3(3) EFZG: **4-week Wartezeit — claim arises only after 4 weeks' uninterrupted employment** (short-tenure employees get Krankengeld from health insurance instead).
- § 12 EFZG: **AU certificate (Arbeitsunfähigkeitsbescheinigung) — since 1.1.2023 (Drittes Bürokratieentlastungsgesetz) the electronic AU (eAU) is mandatory for statutory-insured employees: the employer must retrieve the eAU from the health insurer; the paper "Krankenschein" duty from the 4th day is abolished for eAU-covered employees** (still applies to privately insured). Contract clauses requiring a certificate from day 1 remain valid and common. Sources: `https://www.heuking.de/de/news-events/newsletter-fachbeitraege/artikel/die-elektronische-au-bescheinigung-kommt-drittes-buerokratieentlastungsgesetz-was-gilt-ab-dem-1-januar-2023.html`, `https://www.rtl.de/cms/wichtige-aenderung-bei-der-krankmeldung-ab-januar-2023-wird-die-elektronische-arbeitsunfaehigkeitsbescheinigung-pflicht-5017930.html`
- § 4 EFZG: employer's claim against health insurer (Ausgleichsverfahren, U1).
- § 12(1) EFZG text verified: `https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/entgfg/__3.html`

### 2.7 ArbZG (Arbeitszeitgesetz — Working Time Act)
- § 2 ArbZG: working time definition (Zeit von Beginn bis Ende der Arbeit ohne Ruhepausen).
- § 3 ArbZG: **max 8 hours/day; extension to 10 hours allowed only if within 6 calendar months or 24 weeks the average is 8 hours (Ausgleich)**.
- § 5 ArbZG: **Ruhezeit (rest period) 11 consecutive hours** between work periods.
- § 6 ArbZG: night work (20–6 h); § 7 ArbZG: deviations via collective agreement/works agreement.
- §§ 9–11 ArbZG: Sunday and public-holiday rest as a rule; exceptions (§ 10: continuous operations, emergencies); § 11: compensatory rest days.
- § 14 ArbZG: exceptions (e.g. agriculture, hospitals).
- **Arbeitszeiterfassung (time recording):** BAG 13.9.2022 – 1 ABR 22/21 (ECLI:DE:BAG:2022:130922.B.1ABR22.21.0): employer must record **start, end and duration of daily working time (including overtime)** — derived from § 3(2) No. 1 ArbSchG read with Art. 31(2) EU Charter and Directive 2003/88/EC (following EuGH 14.5.2019 – C-55/18 "CCOO"); applies to all employees, including Gleitzeit (flex-time) and Vertrauensarbeitszeit (trust-based hours); no exception for white-collar. Official text: `https://www.bundesarbeitsgericht.de/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/1-ABR-22-21.pdf`, decision page `https://www.bundesarbeitsgericht.de/entscheidung/1-abr-22-21`
- **2025/2026 reform status:** no statute yet. The BAG's duty rests on ArbSchG, not ArbZG. A Referentenentwurf (BMAS, announced ~June 2026) proposes electronic recording in the ArbZG with a Verordnungsermächtigung for exceptions and a Tariföffnungsklausel — **not yet enacted as of 2026-08-22**. Sources: `https://www.eversheds-sutherland.com/de/germany/insights/update-zum-arbeitszeitgesetz-referentenent-wurf-zur-elektronischen-arbeitszeiterfassung`, `https://timesec.de/blog/blog-neues-arbeitszeitgesetz-2026/`, `https://efarbeitsrecht.net/arbeitszeitgesetz-2026-mehr-flexibilitaet-oder-mehr-buerokratie-was-arbeitgeber-jetzt-wissen-muessen/`

### 2.8 MiLoG (Mindestlohngesetz — Minimum Wage Act)
- § 1 MiLoG: statutory minimum wage; **from 1.1.2026: €13.90/hour** (Fünfte Mindestlohnanpassungsverordnung, Kabinett 2025; from 2027: €14.60). 2025: €12.82. Official: `https://www.bmas.de/DE/Service/Presse/Pressemitteilungen/2025/mindestlohn-steigt-zum-ersten-januar-2026.html`; Bundesregierung: `https://www.bundesregierung.de/breg-de/aktuelles/mindestlohn-steigt-2391010`
- § 2 MiLoG: **Fälligkeit — wage due no later than the last working day of the month following the work period; for continuous night work, within 4 weeks; no Aufrechnung/Verrechnung except for rent/board and court-seized claims; § 2(2): no discounting (Abschläge) below the minimum**.
- § 3 MiLoG: scope (employees incl. home workers, trainees; excluded: minors, apprentices, certain volunteers).
- § 8/9 MiLoG: **Aufzeichnungspflichten — § 17 MiLoG: employer must record start/end/duration of daily work for employees subject to § 8 MiLoG (low-wage sectors: agriculture, construction, hospitality, transport, etc.); § 9 MiLoG: record-keeping (Belegaufbewahrung) 2 years**.
- § 20 MiLoG: **Auftraggeberhaftung — principal/contractor liability: entrepreneurs and principals are liable for their contractors' failure to pay minimum wage (and social-security contributions) like a guarantor (bürgenhaft)**; § 13/14: enforcement by Finanzkontrolle Schwarzarbeit.
- AGB point: an Ausschlussfrist (forfeiture clause) covering minimum-wage claims is void (BAG 18.9.2018 – 9 AZR 162/18; press release: `https://www.bundesarbeitsgericht.de/presse/mindestlohn-arbeitsvertragliche-ausschlussfrist/`).

### 2.9 EntgTranspG (Entgeldtransparenzgesetz — Pay Transparency Act) + EU Directive (EU) 2023/970
- § 3 EntgTranspG: equal-pay principle (Entgeltgleichheit) for equal or equivalent work regardless of gender.
- § 7 EntgTranspG: **Auskunftsanspruch (individual information right)** — employees in establishments with >200 employees may request information on pay criteria and average comparator pay (same/equivalent work, opposite sex), max. every 2 years, from 1.1.2018.
- § 10 EntgTranspG: reporting duties for employers >500 employees (Entgeltberichtsbericht every 5 years; § 21: measures if gender pay gap >...); § 12 EntgTranspG: operation-level review procedures.
- BAG 16.1.2020 – 8 AZR 483/18 (Grundsatzurteil, published 15.7.2020): unjustified refusal of information shifts the burden of proof; BAG 21.1.2021 – 8 AZR 488/19 (comparator group definition). Source: `https://kliemt.blog/2020/07/15/ueber-geld-spricht-man-nicht-oh-doch-neues-urteil-des-bag-zum-auskunftsanspruch-nach-entgtranspg`, `https://www.ordio.com/insights/lexikon/entgelttransparenzgesetz`
- **EU Pay Transparency Directive (EU) 2023/970: transposition deadline 7.6.2026 — NOT met by Germany.** No implementing statute enacted by 2026-08-22 (government bill pending; expert commission recommendations 2025). Consequences: Directive provisions may be directly invoked against the state (horizontal effect debated); enforcement gap. Sources: `https://kpmg-law.de/en/implementation-of-the-pay-transparency-directive-what-the-expert-commission-recommends/`, `https://kliemt.blog/2026/05/26/keine-puenktliche-etrl-umsetzung-in-deutschland-was-gilt-ab-7-juni-2026`, `https://www.deloitte.com/de/de/services/tax/perspectives/eu-entgelttransparenzrichtlinie.html`

### 2.10 AÜG (Arbeitnehmerüberlassungsgesetz — Temporary Agency Work Act)
- § 8 AÜG: **Gleichstellungsgrundsatz (equal pay/conditions) — temporary workers get the essential working conditions including pay of comparable employees at the user undertaking; tarifliche Abweichung possible (sectoral collective agreements may derogate; after 9 months max. deviation; step-up by 15 months max.)**; § 8(5): minimum hourly rates by Verordnung (§ 3a AÜG). Verified text: `https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/a_g/__8.html`
- §§ 9/10 AÜG: **contracts are void (nichtig) if no AÜG permit; § 10: legal fiction — employment is deemed to exist with the user undertaking (Fiktion des Arbeitsverhältnisses mit dem Entleiher)**; § 9(2): fee-splitting prohibition.
- § 17a AÜG: **Überlassungshöchstdauer (max. assignment duration) 18 months to the same user (since 1.4.2017 AÜG reform); collective agreements may set longer periods; exceeding it = employment with user presumed (Fiktion)**. Source: `https://www.haufe.de/personal/arbeitsrecht/reform-der-zeitarbeit/arbeitnehmerueberlassung-dauer-auf-18-monate-begrenzt_76_433684.html`, `https://belonio.de/hr-glossar/arbeitnehmerueberlassungsgesetz/`
- § 13 AÜG: Verleiher must have permit (Erlaubnispflicht); § 17: user must inform the works council and state the assignment is temporary.

### 2.11 AGG (Allgemeines Gleichbehandlungsgesetz — General Equal Treatment Act)
- § 1 AGG: protected grounds (race/ethnic origin, gender, religion/belief, disability, age, sexual identity).
- § 6 AGG: personal scope (employees, applicants, trainees); § 7 AGG: prohibition of discrimination in employment decisions incl. pay and termination; § 12 AGG: employer's duty to prevent discrimination (organizational measures, training).
- § 15 AGG: **Entschädigung (compensation) — up to 3 monthly salaries for non-hiring discrimination; damages; claim deadline 2 months (§ 15(4))**.
- § 22 AGG: **Beweislast (burden of proof) — if the employee proves indications (Indizien) of discrimination, the employer must prove no violation occurred.** Verified text: `https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/agg/__22.html`

### 2.12 GewO (Gewerbeordnung — Trade Regulation Code) and HGB
- § 106 GewO: **Weisungsrecht (employer's right to direct work)** — content, place, and time of work per fair discretion (billiges Ermessen, § 315 BGB), unless otherwise agreed.
- § 109 GewO: **Zeugnis — written certificate on type and duration of employment; on request a qualified certificate (qualifiziertes Zeugnis) covering conduct and performance (Führung und Leistung)**; must be truthful but benevolent. `https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/gewo/__109.html`
- § 110 GewO: **post-contractual non-compete (nachvertragliches Wettbewerbsverbot) — §§ 74–75f HGB apply correspondingly to all employees**. Verified text: `https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/gewo/__110.html`
- HGB §§ 74–75c (Handlungsgehilfen): non-compete valid only if (a) employer has a legitimate business interest (§ 74(1)), (b) **Karenzentschädigung (compensation) of at least 50% of the last contractual remuneration per year (§ 74(2))** — otherwise void (§ 74(3)?? — § 74(2) HGB), (c) **max. 2 years (§ 74a(1) S. 1 HGB)**; (d) restrictions may not unreasonably impede advancement (§ 74a(1) S. 3); § 75 HGB: employer must pay compensation even if it waives the non-compete; § 75a: employee not bound if employer terminates without important cause; § 75c: no compensation if employee terminated without notice for cause. Non-compete for minors void (§ 74a(2)). Sources: `https://www.afa-anwalt.de/arbeitsrecht-ratgeber/wettbewerbsverbot`, `https://kliemt.blog/2016/04/13/nachvertragliches-wettbewerbsverbot-und-salvatorische-klausel/`

### 2.13 BGB § 613a (Betriebsübergang — Transfer of Undertakings)
- § 613a(1) BGB: on transfer of a business/establishment, the new employer steps into all rights and duties under existing employment relationships.
- § 613a(4) BGB: **dismissal for the reason of the transfer is void (Kündigungsverbot)**.
- § 613a(5) BGB: **Unterrichtung (information) — written information about the transfer date, reasons, legal/economic/social consequences, and employee measures**; must be given before the transfer.
- § 613a(6) BGB: **Widerspruchsrecht (right to object) — within 1 month of receipt of proper information; objection can be lost by Verwirkung (waiver) if the employee continues working for the transferee for a long time without objection (BAG 17.10.2013 – 8 AZR 974/12; 12.11.2009 – 8 AZR 530/07)**.
- § 613a(2) BGB: co-liability of old and new employer for obligations accrued before transfer (1 year).

### 2.14 BDSG § 26 + GDPR (Beschäftigtendatenschutz — Employee Data Protection)
- § 26(1) BDSG: **processing of employee data permitted for establishment, implementation or termination of the employment relationship (Vertragszweck)**; § 26(2): consent — "im Rahmen des Beschäftigungsverhältnisses" with strict conditions (freiwillig; no coercion; transparency; revocable); § 26(4): collective agreements may regulate.
- GDPR (EU) 2016/679: Art. 6, 9, 88 GDPR apply; employer as controller must have a legal basis; Art. 88(2) GDPR: Member States may provide more specific rules (Germany: § 26 BDSG).
- Pitfall: blanket consent clauses in the contract for all possible data uses are largely ineffective (voluntariness); monitoring clauses need legal basis, not just consent.

### 2.15 InsO (Insolvenzordnung)
- § 108 InsO: employment relationships continue with the insolvency estate (Masse); § 113 InsO: **the insolvency administrator may terminate with 3 months' notice (Kündigungsfrist 3 Monate) regardless of contractual notice periods; no KSchG social justification needed (but § 125 InsO: mass layoffs)**; claims from before insolvency = Insolvenzforderungen (quota); post-opening wages = Masseverbindlichkeiten (§ 55 InsO); Insolvenzgeld (§§ 165 ff. SGB III) covers 3 months.

## 3. BAG case-law themes an assessor MUST know

All verified via sources below unless marked `[unverified]`.

### 3.1 Ausschlussfristen (forfeiture/claim-bar clauses)
- Valid model: **two-stage clause — claims must be asserted in writing within 3 months, then litigated within 3 months after rejection** (BAG 25.5.2005 – 5 AZR 572/04: in Formulararbeitsverträgen, two-stage clauses valid; minimum period for judicial assertion = 3 months). `https://datenbank.nwb.de/Dokument/207928/`
- **Clauses with a shorter period (<3 months for the first stage) are invalid** (BAG 28.5.2014 – 5 AZR 422/12; that decision concerned equal-pay — the "3-month minimum" rule is confirmed by NWB/Hensche commentary: `https://www.hensche.de/Rechtsanwalt_Arbeitsrecht_Handbuch_Ausschlussfristen.html`).
- **BAG 26.11.2020 – 8 AZR 58/20: a blanket forfeiture clause covering "all claims" is invalid insofar as it also covers claims for intentional breach (Vorsatzhaftung, § 276(3) BGB)** — since 2015 also invalid if it covers minimum-wage claims (BAG 18.9.2018 – 9 AZR 162/18). `https://testphase.rechtsinformationen.bund.de/case-law/KARE600061615`, `https://rsw.beck.de/aktuell/daily/meldung/detail/bag-arbeitsvertragliche-ausschlussklausel-auf-verfall-aller-ansprueche-ist-unwirksam`
- Written-claim requirement: BAG 11.10.2000 – 5 AZR 313/99 — Schriftformerfordernis in tariff clauses satisfied by e-mail (Textform) if the clause doesn't demand more. `https://www.hensche.de/Arbeitsrecht_aktuell_Ausschlussfrist_Schriftform_Geltendmachung_E-Mail_BAG_5AZR888-08.html` — note: BAG 5 AZR 888/08 (7.4.2010) clarified: "schriftlich" in a tariff clause can be satisfied by e-mail only if the wording permits.

### 3.2 Vertragsstrafe (contractual penalty)
- **Penalty clauses in form employment contracts are in principle invalid** — BAG 23.9.2010 – 8 AZR 897/08: § 309 No. 6 BGB applies to employment contracts; exceptions only where employment-law peculiarities justify (e.g. genuine non-compete penalties, BAG 14.8.2007 – 8 AZR 973/06). `https://www.ra-kotz.de/formulararbeitsvertrag_vertragsstrafeklausel_wirksamkeit.htm`
- BAG 4.3.2004 – 8 AZR 196/03: penalty clauses not categorically void but subject to full review; **no geltungserhaltende Reduktion — an excessively high penalty is wholly void** (BAG 24.8.2017 – 8 AZR 378/16; BAG 24.8.2016 – 5 AZR 703/15: penalty exceeding the remuneration due for the notice period is disproportionate). `https://www.hensche.de/Arbeitsvertrag_Allgemeine_Geschaeftsbedingungen_AGB_Vertragsstrafe_Vertragsstrafenklausel_unwirksam_bei_unangemessener_Hoehe_BAG_8AZR196-03-u.html`, `https://lexetius.com/2016,3553`
- Practice: penalty clauses enforceable only if narrowly tailored (specific breach, capped at a proportionate amount) or in individually negotiated contracts.

### 3.3 Überstundenklauseln ("mit dem Gehalt abgegolten")
- **BAG 17.8.2011 – 5 AZR 406/10: a flat clause that all overtime is covered by the salary is invalid under § 307(1) S. 2 BGB (lack of transparency) — the clause must state how many hours are covered (or a percentage of weekly hours)**. `https://www.bundesarbeitsgericht.de/entscheidung/5-azr-406-10/`, `https://www.haufe.de/id/entscheidung/bag-urteil-vom-17082011-5-azr-40610-HI2768191.html`
- BUT: for employees with clearly above-average remuneration ("Besserverdiener"), the invalid clause may be harmless because there is no reasonable expectation of overtime pay (BAG 17.8.2011 – 5 AZR 406/10, obiter; BAG 21.9.2011 – 5 AZR 629/10; BAG 16.5.2012 – 5 AZR 347/11). Threshold: salary clearly exceeding the Beitragsbemessungsgrenze of the pension insurance or comparable high earners — there is no fixed statutory threshold, but BAG 22.2.2012 – 5 AZR 765/10 requires the clause to define the scope precisely. `https://www.iww.de/sr/archiv/arbeitsrecht-verguetungspflicht-von-ueberstunden-f73472`, `https://anwaltspraxis-magazin.de/fachbeitraege/arbeitsrecht/2021/10/11/neues-zur-ueberstundenverguetung/`
- Valid alternative: "Überstunden bis zu 10 Stunden monatlich sind mit dem Gehalt abgegolten" (specific cap).

### 3.4 Gratifikations-Rückzahlungsklauseln (bonus clawback)
- **Binding period rules (BAG case law, "Richtwerte"): for Gratifikationen under 1 monthly salary, a binding/repayment period until 31.3. of the following year is acceptable; for larger bonuses up to ~1 monthly salary, max. 6 months; the clawback must be time-proportional (zeitanteilige Staffelung)** — otherwise the clause is invalid (no geltungserhaltende Reduktion). BAG 25.4.2007 – 10 AZR 634/06 (€100 = gering); BAG 24.10.2007 – 10 AZR 825/06 (under one monthly salary → only until 31.3.). `https://www.haufe.de/personal/haufe-personal-office-platin/gratifikation-rueckzahlungsklauseln-22-bindungslaenge_idesk_PI42323_HI15972237.html`
- **Clawbacks for employer-initiated termination (betriebsbedingte Kündigung) are void** (BAG 9.6.1993 – 10 AZR 529/92; BAG 13.5.2015 – 10 AZR 266/14). `https://www.arbeitsrechtsiegen.de/artikel/arbeitnehmer-gratifikation-zulaessigkeit-einer-rueckzahlungsklausel/`
- For training-cost clawbacks: max binding 6 months (≤1 month training), 12 months (≤2 months), 2 years (3–4 months), 3 years (6–12 months), 5 years (>2 years); time-proportional reduction mandatory. `https://www.thorn-arbeitsrecht-muenchen.de/lexikon/rueckzahlungsklausel`

### 3.5 Befristungskontrolle (fixed-term review)
- § 14(2) TzBfG: 2 years / 3 extensions (see 2.3); § 14(2) S. 2: **prior employment with the same employer blocks sachgrundlose Befristung** — after BVerfG 6.6.2018 (1 BvL 7/14, 1 BvR 1375/14) the "3-year gap" rule is dead: BAG 23.1.2019 – 7 AZR 733/16 (8 years → still prohibited), BAG 21.8.2019 – 7 AZR 452/17 (22 years → allowed). `https://www.bundesarbeitsgericht.de/entscheidung/7-azr-733-16/`, `https://rsw.beck.de/aktuell/daily/meldung/detail/bag-sachgrundlose-befristung-bei-22-jahre-zurueckliegender-vorbeschaeftigung-zulaessig`, `https://www.cms.law/de/deu/legal-updates/bag-rechtsprechungsaenderung-keine-sachgrundlose-befristung-bei-acht-jahre-zurueckliegender-vorbeschaeftigung`
- The spec's "BAG 23.8.2023 – 7 AZR 308/22" could not be verified; the 7th Senate decided on 16.8.2023 – 7 AZR 300/22 (oral anticipation of start date does not satisfy Schriftform: `https://www.bundesarbeitsgericht.de/entscheidung/7-azr-300-22/`) and 12.6.2024 – 7 AZR 203/23 (Vorbeschäftigung: prior employment of a different kind still blocks sachgrundlose Befristung — `https://www.bundesarbeitsgericht.de/entscheidung/7-azr-203-23/`). Any "7 AZR 308/22" claim = `[unverified]`.
- Kettenbefristung (chain of fixed terms): only the formal limits of § 14(2) apply for sachgrundlos; chains with Sachgrund are reviewed under § 14(1) and the principle of Rechtsmissbrauch (institutional abuse; BAG 17.5.2017 – 7 AZR 420/15).
- Klagefrist: § 17 TzBfG / § 21 TzBfG i.V.m. § 7 KSchG — 3 weeks from the agreed end.

### 3.6 Post-contractual non-compete without compensation
- **Void.** § 110 GewO i.V.m. §§ 74, 74a HGB: no Karenzentschädigung → nichtig. **BAG 22.3.2017 – 10 AZR 448/15: even a salvatorische Klausel cannot cure the missing compensation** (the clause remains void; the contract survives). `https://www.bundesarbeitsgericht.de/entscheidung/10-azr-448-15/`, `https://www.lto.de/recht/nachrichten/n/bag-10-azr-448-15-wettbewerbsverbot-ohne-entschaedigung-unwirksam`, `https://www.haufe.de/personal/arbeitsrecht/wettbewerbsverbot-ohne-karenzentschaedigung-bleibt-unwirksam_76_406280.html`
- **>2 years → void** (§ 74a(1) S. 1 HGB). **<50% compensation → void** (§ 74(2) HGB). Compensation must be paid even if the employer waives the non-compete (§ 75 HGB); employee free if employer terminated without cause (§ 75a HGB) or employee terminated for cause (§ 75b HGB). `https://www.afa-anwalt.de/arbeitsrecht-ratgeber/wettbewerbsverbot`

### 3.7 Zeugnis: "wohlwollende Beurteilung"
- § 109 GewO (certificate), § 630 BGB (reference duty), § 6 Zeugnisrichtlinien; BAG 21.6.2005 – 9 AZR 352/04: certificate must be **wahrheitsgemäß und verständig wohlwollend** (truthful and reasonably benevolent); BAG 23.11.2017 – 6 AZR 683/16 (BAGE 161, 111): content standards confirmed. `https://datenbank.nwb.de/Dokument/208538/`, `https://www.bag-urteil.com/23-11-2017-6-azr-683-16/`
- BAG 9.4.2019 – 9 AZR 227/11: no entitlement to a specific grade ("sehr gut"), but to a fair, individualized assessment; the employer may not include extraneous/personal remarks (BAG 20.8.2019 – 9 AZR 478/18: no private opinions in the certificate). `https://www.bundesarbeitsgericht.de/entscheidung/9-azr-227-11`
- **Zeugnis waivers in the contract (e.g. "kein Anspruch auf qualifiziertes Zeugnis") are ineffective** — § 109(2) GewO mandatory on request; § 630 BGB cannot be waived in advance (§ 619 BGB analogy; general AGB review § 307 BGB).

### 3.8 Urlaubsübertragung at 15 months (EU law)
- See 2.5: BAG 19.2.2019 – 9 AZR 423/16; BAG 7.7.2020 – 9 AZR 245/19 (A); BAG 12.10.2021 – 9 AZR 577/20 (A); BAG 10.3.2021 – 9 AZR 199/20 (long-term sick: 15 months without employer action). EU: C-684/16 (Max-Planck), C-214/10 (Schulte). `https://www.etl-rechtsanwaelte.de/aktuelles/zur-15-monats-verfallfrist-bei-einem-dauerkranken/`

### 3.9 Probezeit (probation)
- **Max 6 months** (§ 622(3) BGB: during an agreed probation period of at most 6 months, notice = 2 weeks). Longer probation clauses are void as to the excess (§ 134 BGB) — the 2-week notice applies only within 6 months. `https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__622.html`
- Fixed-term during probation: sachgrundlose Befristung for Erprobung is possible with Sachgrund (§ 14(1) No. 5 TzBfG: "Befristung zur Erprobung" — objective reason, no 2-year cap); a probation fixed-term must not be an evasion.
- Probation is mandatory-info under NachwG § 2(1) No. 6.

### 3.10 Teilzeitverlangen (§ 8 TzBfG, § 9a)
- § 8 TzBfG: reduction right (employer >15 employees, 3-month lead, refusal only for operational reasons; automatic deemed grant if not answered within 1 month — § 8(5) TzBfG; Fiktion). `https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/tzbfg/__8.html`
- § 9a TzBfG: Brückenteilzeit (see 2.3; verified text above). BAG 7.9.2021 – 9 AZR 595/20: a reduction application that misses the announcement deadline does not trigger the fiction — the claim fails; `https://dejure.org/dienste/vernetzung/rechtsprechung?Gericht=BAG&Datum=07.09.2021&Aktenzeichen=9%20AZR%20595%2F20`; new: BAG 27.1.2026 – 9 AZR 32/25 (tariff limitation of part-time) `https://dejure.org/dienste/vernetzung/rechtsprechung?Gericht=BAG&Datum=27.01.2026&Aktenzeichen=9%20AZR%2032%2F25`.

### 3.11 Arbeitszeiterfassung (BAG 13.9.2022 – 1 ABR 22/21)
- See 2.7. Duty to record start/end/duration for ALL employees (no Vertrauensarbeitszeit exception). `https://www.bundesarbeitsgericht.de/entscheidung/1-abr-22-21` (PDF: `https://www.bundesarbeitsgericht.de/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/1-ABR-22-21.pdf`)

### 3.12 Kündigungsschutz thresholds and Wartezeit
- § 1(1) KSchG (Wartezeit >6 months) + § 23(1) KSchG (>10 employees, see 2.4); part-time weighting; trainees excluded. `https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/kschg/__23.html`, `https://www.arbeitsrechtsiegen.de/artikel/kleinbetriebsklausel-beschaeftigtenanzahl-bei-leiharbeitnehmern-und-praktikant`

### 3.13 Änderungskündigung (§ 2 KSchG)
- See 2.4. BAG practice: an amendment dismissal is justified if the employer could validly terminate and the offered change is proportionate; employee must accept under protest within the 3-week period (§ 2 S. 2 KSchG) or the claim is barred. `https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/kschg/__2.html`

## 5. German-specific pitfalls the skill MUST catch (statutory citation each)

1. **Probezeit >6 Monate** → void excess (§ 622(3) BGB; § 134 BGB); 2-week notice only within 6 months. Flag any probation >6 months or probation without end date.
2. **Fixed-term chains (Kettenbefristung)** → sachgrundlos max 2 years / 3 extensions (§ 14(2) TzBfG); **any prior employment with the same employer blocks sachgrundlose Befristung** (§ 14(2) S. 2 TzBfG; BAG 23.1.2019 – 7 AZR 733/16); Schriftform required (§ 14(4) TzBfG); 3-week Klagefrist (§ 17 TzBfG). Flag chains, renewals past 2 years, and any Vorbeschäftigung (even years ago; 22-year gap is the only recognized exception).
3. **Flat overtime clauses ("Überstunden mit dem Gehalt abgegolten")** → invalid without a defined cap (§ 307(1) S. 2 BGB; BAG 17.8.2011 – 5 AZR 406/10). Only harmless for very high earners (no Vergütungserwartung). Flag "abgegolten" without hour cap; also § 2(1) No. 7 NachwG (remuneration composition must be documented).
4. **Missing NachwG terms** → § 2 NachwG (full catalog in 2.2); Beweislast reversal for undocumented terms (§ 4 NachwG); fines up to €2,000 (§ 5 NachwG). Flag contracts lacking any of the 15 mandatory items (esp. remuneration breakdown, working time, probation, termination procedure + Kündigungsschutzklage deadline).
5. **Ausschlussfristen too short** → first-stage period <3 months invalid (BAG 25.5.2005 – 5 AZR 572/04); blanket "all claims" clauses invalid re: Vorsatz (§ 276(3) BGB; BAG 26.11.2020 – 8 AZR 58/20) and minimum wage (BAG 18.9.2018 – 9 AZR 162/18). Flag <3-month clauses, single-stage clauses, clauses without written-claim requirement.
6. **Non-compete without Karenzentschädigung / >2 years** → void (§ 110 GewO i.V.m. §§ 74, 74a HGB; BAG 22.3.2017 – 10 AZR 448/15); compensation <50% void (§ 74(2) HGB). Flag any post-contractual non-compete lacking ≥50% compensation or exceeding 2 years.
7. **Vertragsstrafe (penalty clauses)** → in form contracts generally void (§ 309 No. 6 BGB via § 310(4) BGB; BAG 23.9.2010 – 8 AZR 897/08); no geltungserhaltende Reduktion (BAG 4.3.2004 – 8 AZR 196/03). Flag penalties in form contracts; only narrow individual-agreement penalties (e.g. non-compete breach) may survive.
8. **Unfettered bonus discretion** → "Bonus nach freiem Ermessen / Freiwilligkeitsvorbehalt ohne Rechtsbindung" clauses: bonus promise with employer discretion must be exercised per billiges Ermessen (§ 315 BGB); setting to zero without justification is invalid; courts fix the amount (BAG 13.5.2015 – 10 AZR 266/14; BAG 3.8.2016 – 10 AZR 710/14). `https://www.heuking.de/de/news-events/newsletter-fachbeitraege/artikel/bonuszahlung-nach-billigem-ermessen.html`, `https://www.twobirds.com/de/insights/2016/germany/october/festlegung-des-bonus-nach-billigem-ermessen`; new BAG 19.2.2025 – 10 AZR 57/24 (NZA 2025, 702). Flag pure "freiwillig/ohne Rechtsanspruch" bonus clauses.
9. **Zeugnis waivers** → ineffective (§ 109(2) GewO; § 630 BGB); flag "Verzicht auf Zeugnis" or "nur einfaches Zeugnis" clauses. Flag clauses that dictate a specific grade (must be truthful; BAG 21.6.2005 – 9 AZR 352/04).
10. **IP clauses conflicting with ArbEG (Arbeitnehmererfindungsgesetz)** → employee inventions are governed by ArbnErfG: Diensterfindungen must be reported (§ 5 ArbnErfG); employer may claim them (§ 6) and must pay reasonable compensation (§ 9 ArbnErfG); blanket "all IP belongs to employer" clauses cannot displace the statutory scheme; free inventions remain the employee's (§ 18 ArbnErfG). `https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/arbnerfg/BJNR007560957.html`, `https://www.ipwiki.de/arbeitnehmererfinderrecht:verguetungsanspruch` — note: the planned ArbEG reform (2024/25 draft on § 40 ff. — duty of disclosure, remuneration) **died with the Bundestag dissolution (Diskontinuität); status as of 2026-08: reform not enacted, ArbnErfG 1957 as amended 2009 applies** (`[verified: draft died; no new enactment found]`; `https://www.lexology.com/library/detail.aspx?g=462ef6a1-ac63-4cca-a0d5-11b83a25168b`).
11. **Data-protection consent clauses** → blanket consent to unlimited processing in the contract is largely ineffective; § 26(2) BDSG requires specific, informed, voluntary consent; employer's legitimate purposes are covered by § 26(1) BDSG anyway; monitoring clauses need legal basis (Art. 88 GDPR, § 26 BDSG; BetrVG § 87(1) No. 6 co-determination for technical monitoring). Flag open-ended consent/data clauses, especially for monitoring, health data, or transfer to third countries.
12. **Garden-leave restrictions (Freistellungsklauseln)** → full-pay garden leave is permissible; but clauses releasing from work *without* pay or requiring the employee to remain available without pay are void (pay continues; §§ 611a, 615 BGB — Annahmeverzug; § 2 EFZG/§ 616 BGB analogies). Flag "unbezahlte Freistellung auf Verlangen des Arbeitgebers" or "Freistellung unter Anrechnung auf Resturlaub ohne Zustimmung" issues (Anrechnung auf Urlaub requires § 7 BUrlG compliance; BAG 10.2.2015 – 9 AZR 53/14: employer can unilaterally set leave during Freistellung only if real possibility of vacation use).
13. **Blanket secondary-employment bans (Nebentätigkeitsverbote)** → a general prohibition is void (Art. 12 GG; § 106 GewO); only justified bans (competition, conflict of interest, working-time limits § 8 ArbZG) are valid; permission-based clauses (Zustimmungsvorbehalt) must leave discretion for refusal to a legitimate reason (BAG 11.12.2001 – 9 AZR 464/00: clause requiring consent for ANY secondary employment is void; BAG 19.12.2019 – 6 AZR 23/19). Flag "jegliche Nebentätigkeit bedarf der Zustimmung" / "Nebentätigkeit ist untersagt" without qualifiers.
14. **Unlawful salary-deduction/Verrechnung clauses** → § 394 BGB (no set-off against wage except lawful claims), § 400 BGB (no assignment), § 850 ff. ZPO (attachment limits), MiLoG § 2(2) (no set-off below minimum wage), EFZG § 6 (no deduction during sick pay — Aufrechnungsverbot). Flag "Aufrechnung mit allen Gegenansprüchen", "Verrechnung von Schäden mit dem Gehalt", "Abzüge bei verspäteter Krankmeldung" (void; only damages claim). § 307 BGB review too.
15. **Choice-of-law/jurisdiction clauses** → Rome I Reg. (EG) 593/2008 **Art. 8(1): parties may choose the applicable law, but the employee keeps the protection of the mandatory rules of the law that would apply absent choice — the law of the habitual workplace (Art. 8(2)) or, failing that, the engaging place of business (Art. 8(3)); Art. 8(4): closest-connection override**; Art. 21 Brüssel Ia (EU) 1215/2012: employee may always sue in the courts of the place where the employee habitually works. Flag clauses choosing foreign law/forum for employees habitually working in Germany — they cannot strip German mandatory protections (MiLoG, ArbZG, BUrlG, KSchG etc.). `https://dejure.org/gesetze/Rom-I-VO/8.html`
16. **Kündigung ohne Schriftform** → § 623 BGB: termination only valid in writing (paper, wet signature); e-mail/Textform void. Flag "Kündigung per E-Mail", "mündliche Kündigung", termination clauses waiving Schriftform.
17. **Ausschlussfrist / notice for Kündigungsschutzklage not in contract** → § 2(1) No. 14 NachwG (mandatory info: 3-week deadline; § 7 KSchG applies even if not documented) — the skill should flag contracts that omit the termination procedure info but should NOT rely on it to extend the deadline (BAG: § 7 KSchG applies regardless; BAG 12.12.2019 – 2 AZR 236/19 on insufficient notice info).
18. **Weisungsrecht / Arbeitsortklauseln "bundesweit einsetzbar"** → § 106 GewO limits direction to fair discretion; unilateral mobility clauses ("Einsatzort beliebig") need a defined scope, otherwise § 307(1) BGB (BAG 13.4.2010 – 9 AZR 36/09: broad Versetzungsklauseln reviewed under § 106 GewO; clear limits required). Flag open-ended "bundesweit/weltweit einsetzbar" without travel-cost or notice rules.
19. **Wettbewerbsverbot während der Beschäftigung + Vertragsstrafe** → during employment the duty of loyalty (§ 60 HGB for Handlungsgehilfen; § 241(2) BGB) applies; penalty clauses for breach of the in-employment non-compete are the one recognized exception to the AGB penalty ban (BAG 14.8.2007 – 8 AZR 973/06) — but only if proportionate.
20. **Feiertags- und Nachtzuschläge, Urlaubsentgelt** → clauses excluding supplements must respect § 2 EFZG (holiday pay) and § 11 BUrlG (Urlaubsentgelt = 13-week average); flag "keine Zuschläge" or flat all-inclusive pay that falls below statutory minimums.
