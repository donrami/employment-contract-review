## 1. Sachbezugswerte 2026 (SvEV) — CONFLICT RESOLVED → VERIFIED

**Pinned values for calendar year 2026:**
- **Verpflegung (full board): 345 €/month** — § 2(1) SvEV: Frühstück 71 € + Mittagessen 137 € + Abendessen 137 € = 345 €.
- **Unterkunft (lodging): 285 €/month** — § 2(3) SvEV.
- Daily values: Frühstück 2.37 €, Mittag- und Abendessen je 4.57 € (2025: 2.30 / 4.40).

**Resolution of the 333/345 and 282/285 conflict:** 333 €/282 € were the **2025** values; 345 €/285 € are the **2026** values. Secondary sources citing 333/282 for 2026 are stale/incorrect. Confirmed via three independent primary/authoritative sources:

1. **Gesetze-im-internet.de, § 2 SvEV (consolidated, current version):** Verpflegung "auf monatlich 345 Euro" (71+137+137), Unterkunft "auf monatlich 285 Euro". https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/svev/__2.html — accessed 2026-08-22.
2. **16. Verordnung zur Änderung der Sozialversicherungsentgeltverordnung (SvEV) vom 19.12.2025, BGBl. I Nr. 377** (legal basis for the 2026 figures) — cited verbatim in MB.NRW 2026 Nr. 32, Bekanntmachung des Ministeriums der Finanzen NRW vom 28.01.2026 (issued 05.02.2026), which lists daily meal values 2.37/4.57 € for 2026. https://recht.nrw.de/mbnrw/2026-32 and PDF https://recht.nrw.de/system/files/pdf/announcement/2026/01/30/040e8d/sachbezugswerte-nach-der-sozialversicherungsentgel.pdf — accessed 2026-08-22.
3. Corroborating secondary: Taxmaro (2026: 333→345, 282→285 trajectory) https://www.taxmaro.com/post/sachbezugswerte-2026; NWB livefeed (Unterkunft 282→285) https://datenbank.nwb.de/Dokument/1084938/; ad-hoc-news (Unterkunft 285) https://www.ad-hoc-news.de/boerse/news/ueberblick/sachbezugswerte-2026-hoehere-pauschalbetraege-fuer-verpflegung/68509985 — all accessed 2026-08-22.

## 3. Law-drift status (as of 2026-08-22)

### 3a. EU Pay Transparency Directive 2023/970 (ETRL) — transposition MISSED, direct effect for public employers CONFIRMED
- **No German transposition statute enacted.** Transposition deadline 7.6.2026 missed; no Umsetzungsgesetz in force as of the deadline.
- **Direct effect for public employers from 8.6.2026: CONFIRMED.** All state subdivisions (Ministerien/Behörden, Bundeswehr/Polizei, Länderverwaltungen, Gebietskörperschaften, state-owned companies with special powers) must apply the ETRL directly from 8.6.2026. Source: Kliemt Arbeitsrecht blog, "Keine pünktliche ETRL-Umsetzung in Deutschland – Was gilt ab 7. Juni 2026?" (Dr. Till Heimann, 26.05.2026; NZA Heft 9/2026 S. 637). https://kliemt.blog/2026/05/26/keine-puenktliche-etrl-umsetzung-in-deutschland-was-gilt-ab-7-juni-2026 — accessed 2026-08-22.
- **Private employers:** ETRL not directly applicable between private parties, BUT German courts must interpret national law (AGG, EntgTranspG) directive-conformingly from 8.6.2026; equal-pay claims can be based on Art. 157 TFEU (horizontal direct effect). BAG 23.10.2025 – 8 AZR 300/24 ("Paarvergleich") cited as showing lowered hurdles. Same source.
- **Status: NOT TRANSPOSED (in draft/consultation only).** Cabinet agenda item surfaced 27.5.2026 but no bill became law.

### 3b. EU Platform Work Directive 2024/2831 — IN DRAFT, not published
- Deadline: 2.12.2026.
- BMAS "erarbeitet derzeit den Referentenentwurf" — confirmed by German Bundestag hib 344/2026 (24.04.2026), government answer to Kleine Anfrage 21/5454 (questions 21/5124): BMAS preparing the Referentenentwurf, examining subcontractor measures incl. a possible "Direktanstellungsgebot"; deadline 2.12.2026 noted. https://www.bundestag.de/presse/hib/kurzmeldungen-1167478 — accessed 2026-08-22.
- **No published Referentenentwurf found** in searches up to 2026-08-22 (searched BMAS/Bundestag/press). Status: in preparation, no public draft. [No public draft as of access date — further search may find one later in 2026.]

### 3c. ArbZG reform (weekly cap + electronic time recording) — IN DRAFT (Referentenentwurf), NOT enacted
- New BMAS Referentenentwurf circulates (2026, replacing the abandoned 2023 draft); BMAS itself calls it "interne Arbeitsfassung".
- Key contents per Gleiss Lutz update (24.06.2026): electronic recording of Beginn/Ende/Dauer of daily working time as default (§ 16 Abs. 2 ArbZG-E), recording on the day of work; opt-out via Tarifvertrag/Betriebsvereinbarung with recording by 7th calendar day; delegation possible; record-keeping ≥2 years; **weekly instead of daily cap only via Tarifvertrag parties (§ 7 Abs. 1 Nr. 1b ArbZG-E)**, not statutory default; reduced compensation periods (6→4 months/24→16 weeks); fines up to 30,000 € (§ 22 ArbZG-E); transition periods 1–5 years by company size. https://www.gleisslutz.com/de/know-how/bmas-update-neuer-referentenentwurf-zur-aenderung-des-arbeitszeitgesetzes — accessed 2026-08-22.
- **Status: draft only, NOT enacted.** The 8h daily cap in § 3 ArbZG remains in force (RefE leaves § 3 untouched). "Announced June 2026" = the new RefE surfaced ~June 2026; no Kabinett bill found.

### 3d. July-2026 coalition package "Ein Programm für Aufschwung und Beschäftigung" — AGREEMENT ONLY, no bill passed any chamber
- Coalition (CDU/CSU/SPD) agreed **1.7.2026** (published 2.7.2026): 48-month sachgrundlose Befristung for hires until 31.12.2030, up to 6 renewals, relaxed Vorbeschäftigungsverbot (re-hiring same employer); KSchG high-earner relaxation; AU-Bescheinigung ab Tag 1 (telephone sick note abolished); per Punkt 5 of the agreement.
- Primary: Bundeskanzler.de/Bundesregierung PDF "Ein Programm für Aufschwung und Beschäftigung" (Koalitionsausschuss, 02.07.2026). https://www.bundesregierung.de/resource/blob/2196306/2445592/bc8e5e160d879f0bdd593121a96a45d2/2026-07-02-koaausschuss-data.pdf — accessed 2026-08-22.
- **No Gesetzentwurf introduced in Bundestag as of 2026-08-22.** The only Bundestag document found is the Greens' Kleine Anfrage "Mögliche Ausweitung der sachgrundlosen Befristung" (BT-Drs. 21/7226, 17.07.2026), which restates the 1.7.2026 agreement and asks for empirical justification — evidence that no bill has been tabled. https://dserver.bundestag.de/btd/21/072/2107226.pdf — accessed 2026-08-22. Wikipedia "Programm für Aufschwung und Beschäftigung" (3w old, ~early Aug 2026) likewise describes it as a presented reform package (34 Punkte), not an enacted law. **Status: political agreement, not passed any chamber.**
