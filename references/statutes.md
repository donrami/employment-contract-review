**As of 2026-08-22.** Lookup reference for contract assessment under German law (EU overlay lives in `eu-law.md`). Every claim carries its statute section; every section block carries a URL. Annual verification note: currency figures and limits change **1 Jan** (Mindestlohn, BBG, Sachbezugswerte, Versicherungspflichtgrenze) and **1 Jul** (Pfändungsfreigrenzen); re-verify before each review run. ## 1. Norm hierarchy

1. **Zwingendes Recht** (mandatory statutory law) — MiLoG, ArbZG, most of BUrlG/KSchG/MuSchG.
2. **Tarifvertrag** (collective agreement) — § 4 TVG; § 4(3) TVG Günstigkeitsprinzip (TV may deviate in the employee's favor only).
3. **Betriebsvereinbarung** (works agreement) — §§ 77, 87 BetrVG (co-determination on pay structure, working time, technical monitoring § 87(1) No. 6).
4. **Individual contract** — lowest; any clause deviating from mandatory law to the employee's detriment is **void (§ 134 BGB)** or unenforceable. Statutory minimums are floors, not ceilings.

- § 134 BGB (statutory-prohibition voidness): `https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__134.html`
- § 4 TVG: `https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/tvg/__4.html`
- §§ 77, 87 BetrVG: `https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/betrvg/__77.html`, `https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/betrvg/__87.html`

### AGB-Kontrolle (standard-terms review) — §§ 305–310 BGB applied to employment contracts

- § 305(1) BGB: AGB = pre-formulated terms for a multitude of contracts (Formulararbeitsvertrag).
- § 305b BGB: individual agreement (Individualabrede) prevails over AGB.
- § 305c(1) BGB: surprising/overly hidden clauses (überraschende Klauseln) do not become part of the contract.
- § 305c(2) BGB: ambiguity resolves against the user (Unklarheitenregel).
- § 306 BGB: severability — void clause → contract survives; no geltungserhaltende Reduktion in AGB cases; § 306(2) lets statutory law step in.
- § 307(1) BGB: unreasonable disadvantage; § 307(1) S. 2: **Transparenzgebot** (clarity/understandability).
- § 307(2) BGB: deviation from essential statutory principles / core obligations → unreasonable disadvantage presumed.
- § 308 BGB: clauses subject to review; § 309 BGB: clause catalog (e.g. No. 6 penalty clauses).
- **§ 310(4) S. 2 BGB: §§ 305–310 BGB apply to employment contracts**, with employment-law peculiarities ("im Arbeitsrecht geltende Besonderheiten") considered under § 307(1)-(2). Clause-by-clause review; no salvatorische Klausel can cure a void clause.

`https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__305.html` · `__305b.html` · `__305c.html` · `__306.html` · `__307.html` · `__308.html` · `__309.html` · `__310.html`

## 2. BGB (Bürgerliches Gesetzbuch — Civil Code)

- **§ 611a BGB** — employment contract definition (Dienstvertrag mit Weisungsgebundenheit und persönlicher Abhängigkeit). `https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__611a.html`
- **§ 612a BGB** — Maßregelungsverbot: no detriment for exercising rights. `https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__612a.html`
- **§ 615 BGB** — Annahmeverzug: pay continues when employer fails to accept work (basis for garden-leave pay). `https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__615.html`
- **§ 616 BGB** — continued pay for temporary personal obstacles (verhältnismäßig nicht erhebliche Zeit); largely displaced by EFZG/collective agreements. `https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__616.html`
- **§ 619 BGB** — §§ 617, 618 (duty of care) mandatory; cannot be waived in advance (Zeugnis waiver analogy). `https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__619.html`
- **§§ 620–623 BGB** — duration & termination form. § 620: employment ends at term. **§ 623: any Kündigung (and its revocation) valid only in writing (Schriftform — paper, wet signature; e-mail/fax/Textform void; electronic form excluded).** `https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__620.html` · `__623.html`
- **§ 626 BGB** — fristlose Kündigung (extraordinary, no notice) for important cause; § 626(2): 2-week claim period from knowledge of the facts. `https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__626.html`
- **§ 628 BGB** — damages for early termination (e.g. loss of notice-period pay). `https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__628.html`
- **§ 630 BGB** — duty to issue a written reference/Zeugnis. `https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__630.html`
- **§ 613a BGB** — Betriebsübergang (transfer of undertakings):
  - § 613a(1): transferee steps into all rights/duties; § 613a(2): 1-year co-liability old/new employer.
  - § 613a(4): dismissal for the reason of the transfer is **void**.
  - § 613a(5): written information duty before transfer; § 613a(6): 1-month right to object (Widerspruchsrecht).
  `https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__613a.html`
- **§ 622 BGB** — notice periods (Kündigungsfristen) `[verified 2026-08-22]`:
  - § 622(1): base **4 weeks to the 15th or end of calendar month**.
  - § 622(2): employer's extended periods to month-end: 1 month (2 yrs tenure), 2 (5 yrs), 3 (8 yrs), 4 (10 yrs), 5 (12 yrs), 6 (15 yrs), 7 (20 yrs).
  - § 622(3): during an agreed probation of **at most 6 months**, notice = **2 weeks**; longer probation void as to the excess (§ 134 BGB).
  - § 622(5): contractual shortening of statutory (employer) periods void; age-based carve-outs disapplied (C-555/07 Kücükdeveci).
  `https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__622.html`
- **§ 276(3) BGB** — liability for intent (Vorsatz) cannot be waived in advance; blanket forfeiture clauses void insofar as they cover intentional breach. `https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__276.html`
- **§ 315 BGB** — Leistungsbestimmung nach billigem Ermessen: employer discretion (bonus, targets, Versetzung) reviewable; zero-setting without justification invalid. `https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__315.html`
- **§§ 394, 400 BGB** — no set-off against wage except lawful claims (§ 394); wage claims not assignable (§ 400). `https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__394.html` · `__400.html`

## 3. Nachweisgesetz (NachwG — Evidence Act; implements EU Directive 2019/1152)

In force 1.8.2022 (BGBl. I 2022, S. 1170). `https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/nachwg/`

- **§ 2(1) NachwG — 15 mandatory documented items (Niederschrift):** (1) parties' names/addresses; (2) start date; (3) fixed-term end/foreseeable duration; (4) place of work (or multiple/variable); (5) brief activity description; (6) probation period if agreed; (7) **remuneration composition incl. overtime pay, supplements, allowances, bonuses, special payments — each separately — plus due date and payment method**; (8) agreed working time, rest breaks/periods; shift work: system, rhythm, change conditions; (9) Arbeit auf Abruf: on-demand agreement, minimum guaranteed hours, reference period, advance-notice period; (10) possibility of overtime and its conditions; (11) annual leave entitlement; (12) employer-provided training entitlements; (13) occupational-pension provider name/address; (14) **termination procedure: Schriftform requirement, notice periods for both parties, and the 3-week deadline for the Kündigungsschutzklage (unfair-dismissal claim) — § 7 KSchG applies even if info is deficient**; (15) reference to applicable Tarifverträge, Betriebs-/Dienstvereinbarungen, church-law commission rules.
- **§ 2(1) S. 9 — deadlines:** items 1, 7, 8 → **first day of work**; items 2–6, 9, 10 → **7 calendar days**; items 11–15 → **1 month**. Changes documented within 1 month of effect (§ 3).
- **§ 2(2)** — work abroad >4 consecutive weeks: additional items (country, duration, currency, benefits, return terms) before departure; § 2(3): posting items per Directive 96/71/EC.
- **§ 2(5)** — a full written employment contract satisfies the documentation requirement insofar as it contains the required items.
- **§ 4 S. 1 NachwG** — **Beweislast reversal**: if the employer fails to document agreed terms, the employee's assertion of those terms is presumed correct in litigation; claim not time-barred before year-end after termination.
- **§ 5 NachwG** — Ordnungswidrigkeit: fine up to **€2,000 per violation**.
`https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/nachwg/__2.html` · `__4.html` · `__5.html`

## 4. TzBfG (Teilzeit- und Befristungsgesetz — Part-Time and Fixed-Term Act)

- **§ 4 TzBfG** — part-time workers may not be treated less favorably (Diskriminierungsverbot). `https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/tzbfg/__4.html`
- **§ 7 TzBfG** — change-of-hours request; 1-month reasoned reply duty. `https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/tzbfg/__7.html`
- **§ 8 TzBfG** — right to reduce hours: employer **>15 employees**; 3-month lead; refusal only for operational reasons (§ 8(4)); **automatic deemed grant if unanswered within 1 month** (§ 8(5) Fiktion). `https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/tzbfg/__8.html`
- **§ 9 TzBfG** — right to return to longer hours; employer must announce vacancies. `https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/tzbfg/__9.html`
- **§ 9a TzBfG** — Brückenteilzeit (since 1.1.2019): temporary reduction for a fixed 1–5-year period after >6 months' tenure; employer regularly >45 employees; refusal only for operational reasons (quota: 46–200 employees → max. 1 acceptance per 15 employees); no new reduction during the period; re-application earliest 1 year after return. `https://dejure.org/gesetze/TzBfG/9a.html`
- **§ 12 TzBfG** — Arbeit auf Abruf (on-call): minimum guaranteed hours — **20 h/week default fiction**; if hours agreed, at least **20% of agreed weekly hours guaranteed**; fluctuation bands: scheduling >25% above / >20% below agreed hours require consent; reference-period/advance-notice rules (2022 amendment). `https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/tzbfg/__12.html`
- **§ 14(1) TzBfG** — fixed term requires a sachlicher Grund: catalog (temporary need, post-training transition, replacement/Vertretung, nature of work, **probation/Erprobung (No. 5)**, personal reasons, budget-funded, court settlement). `https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/tzbfg/__14.html`
- **§ 14(2) TzBfG** — sachgrundlose Befristung: **max 2 years total, max 3 extensions**; **S. 2: prohibited if any prior employment (fixed or permanent) with the same employer existed** (Vorbeschäftigungsverbot; BVerfG 6.6.2018 – 1 BvL 7/14, 1 BvR 1375/14; BAG 23.1.2019 – 7 AZR 733/16: 8-year gap blocks; BAG 21.8.2019 – 7 AZR 452/17: 22-year gap OK; BAG 12.6.2024 – 7 AZR 203/23: prior employment of a different kind blocks).
- **§ 14(2a)** — new companies: 4 years/multiple extensions; **§ 14(3)** — employees >52 years after ≥4 months' unemployment: 5 years.
- **§ 14(4) TzBfG** — fixed term requires **Schriftform (§ 126 BGB, paper signature; e-mail insufficient)**; § 16: otherwise term void → indefinite contract. `https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/tzbfg/__16.html`
- **§ 15(2) TzBfG** — 3 calendar days' notice during fixed term; § 15(3): unstated term → 2 years presumed; § 15(4): probation in fixed-term contract requires "mit gesetzlicher Frist kündbar". `https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/tzbfg/__15.html`
- **§ 17 TzBfG** — Klagefrist: challenge to the fixed term within **3 weeks** of the agreed end (§ 21 analogizes § 7 KSchG: late filing = term deemed valid). `https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/tzbfg/__17.html`

## 5. KSchG (Kündigungsschutzgesetz — Unfair-Dismissal Protection Act)

- **§ 1 KSchG** — dismissal socially unjustified unless person-, conduct-, or operation-related (personen-, verhaltens-, betriebsbedingt). **§ 1(1): Wartezeit — protection only after >6 months' continuous employment.** `https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/kschg/__1.html`
- **§ 1a KSchG** — severance pattern: **0.5 monthly salaries per year of tenure** if the employer offers severance and waives the claim. `https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/kschg/__1a.html`
- **§ 2 KSchG** — Änderungskündigung: dismissal + offer of modified terms; employee must accept under protest (Vorbehalt) within the 3-week deadline (§ 2 S. 2) or claim is barred. `https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/kschg/__2.html`
- **§ 4 KSchG** — Klagefrist: unfair-dismissal claim within **3 weeks** of receipt of the written termination. `https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/kschg/__4.html`
- **§ 5 KSchG** — late filing only if prevented without fault (nachträgliche Zulassung). `https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/kschg/__5.html`
- **§ 7 KSchG** — after the deadline the dismissal is **deemed valid** (fiktive Wirksamkeit). `https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/kschg/__7.html`
- **§ 23(1) KSchG** — Kleinbetriebsklausel: general protection does NOT apply in establishments regularly employing **≤10 employees** (≤5 for contracts begun before 1.1.2004); part-time weighting: ≤20 h/week = 0.5, ≤30 h/week = 0.75; trainees excluded. `https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/kschg/__23.html`
- **§ 14(2) KSchG** — executives' special termination conditions. `https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/kschg/__14.html`
- **Special protection (besonderer Kündigungsschutz):** MuSchG §§ 17–18 (pregnancy + 4 months after birth; Behördenzustimmung), SGB IX § 168 (severe disability; Integrationsamt consent, §§ 85 ff. SGB IX), BetrVG § 15 (works-council members), ArbPlSchG § 2 (military reserve), PflegeZG § 5 (care leave), BEEG § 18 (parental leave).

## 12. GewO (Gewerbeordnung) and HGB
