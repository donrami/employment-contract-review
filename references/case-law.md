## Citation rule (the hallucination firewall)

Findings MAY cite, and only:

1. **statute sections** (with gesetze-im-internet.de URL where available), or
2. **this whitelist** (case number + court + date as listed here — the whitelist itself is the verified source; no URL required), or
3. **web-verified citations** outside this list, but ONLY with a working URL and the marker `[web-verified YYYY-MM-DD]`.

**Unsourced case numbers are NEVER emitted.** A case the model "knows" but that is absent from this whitelist and lacks a `[web-verified]` URL must not be cited. Known-trap citations are blacklisted below and must never appear in findings.

Note on CJEU URLs: where a slice did not supply a URL, the URL follows the deterministic EUR-Lex CELEX locator (`620YYCJ0NNN` = `https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:620YYCJ0NNN`, with YY = filing year, NNN = case number zero-padded). Entries from the slices keep their original verified URLs.
