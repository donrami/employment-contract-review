## 1. Core terms DE ↔ EN

## 4. Usage notes (subtle-term guardrails)

- **Freistellung**: in employment contracts = release from work (garden leave); in B2B/indemnity clauses = indemnification. Disambiguate by context — misreading voids the finding.
- **Abgeltung**: flat coverage (overtime) vs compensation in lieu (leave). Never translate as "settlement".
- **Ausschlussfrist** ≠ **Verjährung**: contract-based forfeiture vs statutory limitation.
- **Verwirkung** ≠ **Ausschlussfrist**: § 242 BGB time-based loss vs contractual claim-bar.
- **Kündigung** ≠ **Aufhebungsvertrag**: unilateral termination vs mutual agreement.
- **Entgeltfortzahlung** ≠ **Krankengeld**: employer 6-week pay vs insurer benefit.
- **Probezeit** (statutory ceiling 6 months) ≠ **Befristung zur Erprobung** (§ 14(1) No. 5 TzBfG, no 2-year cap).
- **sachgrundlos** (no reason) ≠ **ohne Sachgrundprüfung**: § 14(2) vs § 14(1) TzBfG.
- **Freistellung (garden leave)** ≠ **Urlaub**: leave requires § 7 BUrlG compliance; garden leave does not automatically consume leave.
- **billiges Ermessen** (§ 315 BGB) ≠ **freies Ermessen**: equitable, reviewable discretion — "frei" phrasing in a clause is the red flag.
- **Weisungsrecht** (§ 106 GewO) is bounded by billiges Ermessen — a clause delegating "everything" to employer discretion is a transparency defect.
- **Karenzentschädigung** is compensation for a post-contractual non-compete (≥50%), NOT notice pay — mistranslation as "severance" is a known error.
- **Zeugnis** = employment reference; **Arbeitszeugnis** same; never "testimony" in contract context.
- **Niederschrift** = written record (NachwG) — not "transcript of hearing" in contract context.
- **Vergütung/Entgelt** both = pay; use Entgelt when citing statutes (MiLoG, EntgTranspG).
- **Mindestlohn** figures are date-pinned: €13.90/h (1.1.2026), €14.60/h (1.1.2027), €12.82/h (2025).
- **Befristung ohne Sachgrund** limits: 2 years/3 extensions; pensioner exception § 41(2) SGB VI (8 yrs/12 contracts, since 1.1.2026).
- **Betriebsübergang** applies to asset deals, not share deals — cite § 613a BGB only for the former.
- **Urlaubsentgelt** = 13-week average (§ 11 BUrlG); "base salary only" clauses are the P19 defect.
- **eAU** replaces the paper Krankenschein for statutory-insured (1.1.2023); privately insured still paper.
- **Abfindung** (§ 1a KSchG pattern) ≠ **Karenzentschädigung** (non-compete) — distinct payments, distinct rules.
- **Schwerbehinderung** requires GdB ≥50 (or Gleichstellung); Zusatzurlaub 5 days (§ 208 SGB IX).
- **Zuschläge** (night/Sunday/holiday premiums) are TV-based, not statutory as a rule — but §§ 9–11 ArbZG compensatory rest is statutory.
- **Diensterfindung** vs **freie Erfindung** (ArbnErfG §§ 4, 18): report duty only for the former.
- **Wartezeit** has three different lengths by statute (KSchG 6 months, EFZG 4 weeks, BUrlG 6 months) — always cite the statute.
- **Kleinbetriebsklausel**: ≤10 employees (§ 23(1) KSchG), ≤5 for pre-2004 contracts; part-time weighting 0.5/0.75.
